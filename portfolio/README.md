# Sanam's High-Velocity Software Developer Portfolio

A job-ready, elite portfolio engineered with **pure semantic HTML5 and modern CSS3** (zero JavaScript dependencies), designed specifically to capture the attention of **Big Tech recruiters and hiring managers** at first sight.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Architecture

- **Silicon Valley Dark Glassmorphism Aesthetic**: Inspired by Linear, Vercel, and GitHub.
- **Pure CSS Dynamic Features**:
  - Interactive sticky glassmorphic navigation with active underline animations.
  - Pure CSS mobile hamburger navigation menu (driven by the CSS checkbox hack).
  - Micro-interactions, ambient glow mesh, gradient typography, and hover lift effects.
  - Mobile-first responsive grid & flexbox layouts from 320px screens up to 4K displays.
- **Recruiter Magnet Content Structure**:
  - **Hero Section**: High-impact value proposition, availability status pill, and 4 high-velocity metric counters.
  - **Hackathon Hall of Fame**: Spotlights your **50% Hackathon win rate (2 wins out of 4)** with problem, contribution, and outcome breakdowns.
  - **Technical Arsenal**: Categorized matrix for Python, Full-Stack Web, AI Prompt Engineering, and High-Agency toolsets.
  - **Featured Projects**: Flagship products highlighting AI-native engineering, Python automation, and modern web interfaces.
  - **90-Day Trajectory (Velocity)**: Visual timeline showcasing your rapid curve at Newton School of Technology and why Big Tech should hire you.
  - **Contact & Inquiry Card**: Direct social links and functional mailto form.

---

## 🚀 How to View Locally

Simply double-click `index.html` or open it in your favorite browser:

```bash
# On macOS:
open index.html

# Or start a lightweight local server:
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.

## 🛠️ Personalization Checklist

To replace placeholders with your exact personal details:

1. **Contact Information (`index.html`)**:
   - Replace `sanam@example.com` with your real email address.
   - Replace `linkedin.com/in/sanam-nst` with your real LinkedIn handle.
   - Replace `github.com/sanam-nst` with your real GitHub username.
2. **Resume**:
   - Place your exported resume as `resume.pdf` in this folder, or update the links in `index.html` to point to your Google Drive / Notion resume URL.
3. **Hackathon Names & Projects**:
   - Tweak the project titles, dates, or hackathon names in `#hackathons` and `#projects` to match your exact hackathon victories.
